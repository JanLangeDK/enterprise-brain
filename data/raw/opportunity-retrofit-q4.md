# Retrofit Q4 2026 - Esbjerg pump hall

Opportunity to retrofit the pump hall at Nordvind Energi's Esbjerg site.

- Estimated value: 2.8 MDKK
- Expected decision: December 2026
- Competitor: Sydpumpe A/S
- Champion: Mette Lindgren
- Economic buyer: Jonas Friis
- Depends on the technical specification Mette is drafting, due mid September 2026

Scope discussed so far: variable frequency drives on P-40 and P-22, condition monitoring
with permanent vibration sensors, and an upgrade of the control system interface.

Risk: if the vibration issue on P-40 is still open when the specification is written,
the customer may bundle the fix into the tender and hand the whole scope to Sydpumpe.
