# Site visit, Esbjerg, 14 August 2026

Interviewed Mette Lindgren on site for about 50 minutes, then walked the pump hall with her shift lead.

## What they told us

Mette is worried about pump line P-40. It has been throwing vibration alarms since late June,
roughly twice a week, always under high load. Their own maintenance team replaced the bearings
in July but the alarms came back within ten days. She thinks the issue is alignment, not bearings.

Unplanned downtime on P-40 costs them around 90.000 DKK per event because the digester has to be
throttled. They have had three events this year.

## What they are planning

Nordvind has budget approval for a retrofit of the Esbjerg pump hall in Q4 2026, roughly 2.8 MDKK.
Mette wants variable speed drives and better condition monitoring. The decision goes through
Jonas Friis, but Mette writes the technical specification. She said the specification draft is due
mid September.

Jonas has already asked Sydpumpe A/S for an indicative price. Mette says our response time under
the current SLA is the main reason she would push for us.

## Commitments we made

- Send a vibration analysis proposal for P-40 before 1 September
- Confirm whether the SLA covers alignment work or only bearing replacement
- Bring a condition monitoring demo to the September meeting
