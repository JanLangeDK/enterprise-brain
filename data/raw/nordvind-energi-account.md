# Nordvind Energi A/S

Nordvind Energi A/S is a Danish independent power producer headquartered in Esbjerg.
They operate two biogas-to-power sites and a small district heating plant.
We have been a supplier since 2019, mainly for pumping and heat exchange equipment.

## Key facts

- Customer number in SAP: 100482
- Annual spend with us: approximately 4.2 MDKK
- Account manager: Anders Bak
- Primary technical contact: Mette Lindgren, Operations Manager, Esbjerg site
- Procurement contact: Jonas Friis, Category Manager
- Parent company: Vestkyst Holding ApS

## Sites

- Esbjerg site (main): biogas plant, commissioned 2018, our largest installed base
- Varde site: district heating, smaller, mostly third party equipment

## Commercial notes

Jonas Friis runs a hard tender process and benchmarks us against Sydpumpe A/S every second year.
Mette Lindgren prefers our service quality and has blocked two attempts to switch supplier.
The account has a standing service agreement, Service SLA 2026, signed by Jonas Friis in November 2025.
