# Service SLA 2026 (SLA-2026-0142)

Agreement between Nordvind Energi A/S and our service organisation, effective 1 January 2026,
running for 24 months. Signed by Jonas Friis for the customer.

## Scope

Covers preventive maintenance and corrective repair on all installed pumping equipment at the
Esbjerg site, including pump line P-40 and pump line P-22.

Explicitly included: bearing replacement, seal replacement, impeller inspection, annual overhaul.
Explicitly excluded: shaft alignment services, foundation work, and any modification that changes
the equipment specification.

## Response times

- Critical alarm: on site within 8 working hours
- Non critical: within 3 working days
- Penalty: 15.000 DKK per breach of the critical response time

## Notes

Two critical response breaches in the first half of 2026 are under discussion.
The exclusion of alignment work has not been raised with the customer since signing.
