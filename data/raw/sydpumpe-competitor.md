# Sydpumpe A/S

Regional competitor based in Kolding. Strong on price, weak on service coverage in west Jutland.

- Typically undercuts us by 10 to 15 percent on equipment
- No 8 hour response capability north of Kolding
- Won the Varde district heating scope at Nordvind Energi in 2023
- Currently quoting the Esbjerg pump hall retrofit for Nordvind Energi

Where we win against Sydpumpe it is almost always on response time and installed base knowledge,
not on price.
